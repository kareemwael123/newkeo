Keo Links

Link your accounts once, then just tell Keo what to do: "announce this on my Discord", "publish this site live", "save this as a GitHub gist". Tokens are stored in your own Firebase database under your account.

GitHubnot linked

Full power: create repos & gists, push files, publish sites live on GitHub Pages.

Make a token at github.com/settings/tokens — give it repo permission. Keo only sees what you allow.

Telegramnot linked

Real auto-posting: Keo sends messages to your channel or group through your bot.

Create a bot with @BotFather, add it as admin to your channel/group, then send any message there and use the chat ID.

Discordnot linked

Real auto-posting: Keo posts announcements straight into your channel via webhook.

In Discord: channel settings → Integrations → Webhooks → New Webhook → Copy URL.

Facebook · X · YouTubeone-click ready

These platforms closed their auto-posting APIs, so Keo does the next best thing honestly: he writes the perfect post (or title + description for YouTube), copies it to your clipboard, and opens the platform ready to paste — one click and it's out.

Just ask: "write a Facebook post about our sale and open Facebook" or "prepare a YouTube upload for my new video".
